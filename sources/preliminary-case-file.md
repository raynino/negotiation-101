# DAV Patriot Boot Camp
## Preliminary Case File – All Teams

### Seller Information

**Name:** Evans Outfitters, LLC, an Ohio limited liability company

**Owners:** Ashley Evans (60%) and Terry Guerin (40%)

**Business:** Outdoor adventure outfitters with three locations in the Cincinnati area: Hyde Park, Ohio, Mason, Ohio, and Erlanger, Kentucky. Primary sources of revenue:

- Retail and online sales of fishing, camping, and climbing gear
- Guided fishing, hiking, and climbing trips
- Distribution of Evans Outfitters line of high-end active wear

**History:** Evans Outfitters, LLC (“EO”) was formed in 2015 by Ashley Evans and Terry Guerin. Their first store was located in Hyde Park, Ohio, an affluent neighborhood in the East side of Cincinnati. EO sold high-end outdoor equipment with a specific focus on fly fishing, hiking, and camping gear. The store offered outdoor educational classes as well as guided outdoor trips, both within the local region and to places such as Wyoming and Tennessee. The business was immediately successful.

In 2017, Evans Outfitters opened its second location in Mason, Ohio, a suburb 25 minutes north of downtown Cincinnati. The Mason location offered a much larger retail presence, with more than 4,000 square feet. The additional space allowed EO to expand its retail offerings to kayaking, hunting, and biking products.

A few years later, Evans Outfitters opened its third location in Erlanger, Kentucky because the economy had improved and the Mason store had exceeded revenue expectations. The pandemic hit only a few months after the Erlanger location opened its doors. Although the first few months were difficult, the renewed interest in outdoor activities created by pandemic-related restrictions proved beneficial for EO. The business has stabilized over the past five years, and the company now offers its own brand of apparel, designed by Terry during the pandemic.

### Buyer Information

**Name:** Rocca Holdings, Inc., a Delaware corporation

**Owners:** Rocca Investments, LLC (75%) and Day Equity, LLC (25%)

**Business:** Rocca Holdings, Inc. (“Rocca”) is a privately owned corporation that owns and operates a regional chain of outdoor apparel and equipment stores, known to consumers as “Lucerne.” The Lucerne name is not well-known in the Midwest, as its focus has traditionally been the middle income outdoor market in the Western United States. Its revenue derives from the in-store and online sales of outdoor equipment and apparel.

**History:** Jordan Rocca is the sole owner and member of Rocca Investments, LLC, which is the majority owner of Rocca Holdings. In 2010, Jordan purchased a fledgling retail chain that had overextended its debt. Eschewing the chain’s sports and fitness sales, Jordan refocused the business exclusively on the sale of hunting, fishing, and camping accessories. Rebranded as Lucerne, the chain sought to capture the market for outdoors equipment at an obtainable price. With a new brand, a high-level management team, and a more tailored target market, Lucerne achieved success at an astonishing rate. Within ten years, Rocca operated over 35 Lucerne stores from California to Colorado.

In the fall of 2020, Jordan sold a 25% interest in Rocca to Day Equity, LLC (“Day”), a private equity firm based in Minneapolis. The sale to Day was contingent upon an expansion strategy whereby Rocca would attempt to expand Lucerne from a regional retailer to a national one. The expansion strategy involves opening new Lucerne locations in cities such as Chicago and Kansas City, establishing a new line of high-end outdoor apparel, and acquiring small or mid-sized outdoor outfitters.

### Potential Transaction

**Buyer:** The Day expansion strategy is led by Robin Brozost, an experienced retail sales consultant hired by Rocca in 2024. Brozost has traveled throughout the eastern half of the country over the past few years, meeting with (and buying) privately-owned outfitters that are typically family-run. While knowledgeable with regard to outdoor experiences and their particular client base, most of these businesses are not sophisticated in terms of marketing, finance, or business strategy.

**Seller:** EO has been approached by potential buyers from time to time. The suitors have included national chains, private equity firms, and even clients. In recent months, Ashley and Terry have decided to sell if the right deal comes along.

**Meeting:** Last week, the four individuals met (Jordan and Robin on behalf of Rocca, and Ashley and Terry on behalf of Evans Outfitters). The purpose of the meeting was to get to know one another, exchange preliminary information, and explore a potential acquisition of EO by Rocca. The parties have now agreed to meet again and negotiate a Term Sheet.

**Next Steps:** You will play the role of one of the players as Buyer (Jordan/Robin) or Seller (Ashley/Terry). Financial information regarding Evans Outfitters is available below. You will receive additional confidential information for your party as described in the “Buyer-Specific” or “Seller-Specific” case file that will be provided to you. Your task is to review that information, consult with your teammate, and then negotiate a Term Sheet with the counterparty assigned to you. A template Term Sheet will be provided.
